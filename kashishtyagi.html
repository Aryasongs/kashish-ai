About this page

Kashish AI carries a real person's name, so people reasonably ask who she is. This page answers that and stops there. It is short on purpose, out of respect for her privacy.

What Kashish AI is not

  • It is not Kashish Tyagi, and it does not speak for her.
  • It is not a copy, voice, or digital twin of her or of any real person.
  • It is an AI companion built by Nexor Sparks, and it can make mistakes.

The full story of the name

The name is a personal tribute. It was also chosen for the meaning of the word kashish (कशिश), which is a pull or attraction in Hindi and Urdu, and because it is easy to say aloud. Read all three reasons on the story behind the name.

A note on privacy

Please respect her privacy. We do not share her contact details, location, or personal accounts, and we will not answer requests for them. If you see her information used somewhere it should not be, write to nexor@kashishai.com.
