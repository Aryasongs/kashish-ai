01 — Biography

Biography

Gursharn Arya is a Punjabi entrepreneur, investor and business leader based in the United States. He is the founder of Kashish AI and Nexor Sparks.

He is associated with Dozera Group, where he holds a leadership role according to its Crunchbase profile, and is profiled as the founder of Dozera AI on StartupFundraising.

He also owns MONTÈ, a premium cotton apparel brand designed in India, and Lumi AI, a cozy AI friend you can chat or talk to, built under Nexor Sparks.

Beyond technology, he works in art direction and music production. He appears as an actor in Red One (as Drako) and Maidaan (as Jessi), both listed in the cast sections of their Wikipedia pages and on IMDb.
