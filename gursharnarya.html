About

Biography.

Gursharn Arya is the founder of Kashish AI and Nexor Sparks and is associated with Dozera Group, where he holds a leadership role according to its Crunchbase profile. He is also profiled as the founder of Dozera AI on StartupFundraising.

Beyond technology, Gursharn works in art direction and music production. His film credits are listed on IMDb, including work on Red One and Maidaan.

Kashish AI and Nexor Sparks form the core of his current work: an AI product and a platform for social networking, messaging and creator tools.
