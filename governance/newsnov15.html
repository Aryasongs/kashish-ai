Nexor Chairman tells employees: leave if you want, and take an iPhone 18 Pro Max and a MacBook with you

Summary

After a three-hour meeting with the Board, Nexor’s Founder and Chairman told all 567 employees they are free to leave. Anyone who goes receives an iPhone 18 Pro Max and a MacBook. So far, 10 employees have taken the offer.

LOS ANGELES, California, United States – November 15, 2026 – Nexor, a Kashish AI company, today announced that its Founder and Chairman has told the company’s entire workforce that any employee who wishes to leave is free to do so. Every employee who chooses to depart will receive an iPhone 18 Pro Max and a MacBook from the company.

The announcement followed a three-hour meeting between the Chairman and the Nexor Board of Directors, where the company’s direction, culture and commitment were discussed in depth.

A clear message to the Nexor team

Addressing employees, the Chairman said Nexor wants every person on the team to be there by choice. Anyone who feels Nexor is no longer the right place for them can leave without friction and will be thanked for their contribution.

“Nexor is built by people who want to build it. If you want to go, you are free to go, and we will send you off with respect, an iPhone 18 Pro Max and a MacBook.”
[Founder Name], Founder and Chairman, Nexor

What departing employees receive

Each employee who resigns will receive one iPhone 18 Pro Max and one MacBook. The offer applies across all departments and roles. To date, 10 of 567 employees, about 1.76 percent of the workforce, have taken up the offer.

Management response

Nexor leadership said that more than 98 percent of the team remaining shows strong confidence in the company’s mission. The Board has reaffirmed its support for the Chairman’s decision and expects Nexor to continue executing on its roadmap without interruption.

“We respect every decision our colleagues make. The people who stay are fully committed, and that is the foundation we will grow on.”
Nexor Board of Directors

Industry impact

The move is among the most unconventional workforce announcements by a technology company in recent years. While leading tech firms have long used incentives to attract and retain talent, few have offered premium hardware to people who decide to leave. Industry observers are expected to follow how the policy affects hiring, morale and retention at Nexor in the coming quarters.
