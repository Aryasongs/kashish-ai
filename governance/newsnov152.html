Kashish AI and Nexor Technologies today announced they will not adopt the term “Super Intelligence” (“SI”) and will continue to call the technology what it has always been called: AI. The decision was announced openly by Founder and CEO Gursharn Arya, who leads both companies.

What this is responding to

  • In late September 2026, the White House published a fact sheet on an executive order directing federal agencies to use “Super Intelligence” and “SI” in official communications instead of “Artificial Intelligence” and “AI.”
  • According to CIO.com, the rename covers official correspondence, websites, reports and other non-statutory documents, and a proposed federal definition is due by November 28.
  • The San Francisco Standard reported that leading labs have not relabeled their models as SI.

The order applies to the executive branch. It does not legally bind private companies, which remain free to use their own terminology.

What changes at Kashish AI and Nexor

AIProducts, documentation and marketing at both companies keep the name “AI.”
BUILDThe roadmap does not move. Shipping is the response.
OPENBoth companies will keep engaging with policymakers on substance, including safety, infrastructure and workforce questions.

The point

Names describe technology. They do not build it. Kashish AI and Nexor Technologies will keep putting their energy into the work: the models, the products and the people behind them.

Questions, answered

Will Kashish AI or Nexor use “SI”?

No. Both companies will continue to use “AI” everywhere.

Who made the statement?

Gursharn Arya, Founder and CEO of Kashish AI and CEO of Nexor Technologies.

Does the order apply to private companies?

No. It directs the U.S. executive branch. Private companies set their own terminology.
