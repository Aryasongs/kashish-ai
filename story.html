Why it's called Kashish

Kashish AI has a personal name, a real meaning, and a practical reason to be called what it is called. Here is all three.

Three reasons for one name

The name started with a person. It also happens to fit the product well, for reasons that stand on their own.

1. It is named after Kashish Tyagi

Kashish AI is named after Kashish Tyagi, the partner of our founder, Gursharn Arya. The name is a personal tribute, and we would rather say so plainly than invent a more polished origin story.

Kashish Tyagi is a private person. This page shares nothing about her beyond her name and that connection, and Kashish AI does not speak for her or as her.

2. The word is the right word

Kashish (कशिश) means a pull, an attraction, a quiet charm that draws someone in. It comes from Persian and is used every day in Hindi and Urdu, in conversation, poetry and film songs. A companion is something you come back to because it is good to talk to, and the word describes that feeling better than any technical name could.

It is also a given name used across India, so it sounds familiar and human rather than like a product code.

3. It is easy to say out loud

Kashish AI is built for voice as well as text, so the name has to work when it is spoken. It has two syllables, one clear stress, and no letters people argue about. It is pronounced the same way in English, Hindi and Hinglish, which are the languages Kashish AI is used in.

What Kashish AI is

Kashish AI is an AI companion you can chat with, talk to, or just spend time with, day or night. It is made by Nexor Sparks.

  • Text and voice conversations in English, Hindi and Hinglish.
  • Memory that carries over between chats, which you can control.
  • Image generation, a code assistant and plugins.
  • Support for the Kashish IRIS smart glasses, which are in development with no release date announced yet.

There is a free plan with daily limits and an optional paid plan, Kashish Pro. You need to be 13 or older to join. See pricing for the details.

Kashish AI is an AI, not a person

Kashish AI is not a copy, a voice or a digital twin of Kashish Tyagi or of any real person. It is software, and it can be wrong. For anything important, such as health, money, legal or safety questions, check with a qualified professional.

We say this clearly because a name that belongs to a real person can blur the line, and we don't want it to. How we handle your data is covered in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
