Official Announcement Regarding Anmol Sunda

Nexor Sparks and Kashish AI announce an organizational change concerning Anmol Sunda, effective September 20, 2026.

Published September 20, 2026 Issued by Nexor Sparks & Kashish AI Official Communication
Official Statement

Nexor Sparks, the parent organization of Kashish AI, and Kashish AI officially announce that Anmol Sunda is no longer associated with Kashish AI, effective September 20, 2026.

Organizational Structure

Kashish AI operates within the Nexor Sparks organization. Accordingly, organizational decisions made at the parent-organization level may affect associated positions and responsibilities within Kashish AI.

The Announcement

Nexor Sparks and Kashish AI have formally recorded the end of Anmol Sunda's association with Kashish AI effective September 20, 2026.

Following the parent-organization decision, Anmol Sunda is no longer part of the Kashish AI organizational structure from the effective date stated above.

This announcement is intended to provide a clear public record of the organizational change and the effective date associated with it.

Leadership

Kashish AI identifies Gursharn Arya as its Founder & Chairman.

The organization continues its technology, product development, and business activities under its established leadership and organizational structure.
