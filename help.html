Managing parental controls in Kashish AI

Learn how to link a teen account, manage parental controls, and understand exactly what protections apply today — and what they don't cover yet.

Updated: September 2026

Overview

Parental controls let a parent or guardian link their Kashish AI account with a teen's account, review which protections are on, and approve or decline the teen's requests to remove specific ones. Either person can unlink the accounts at any time.

Linking accounts does not give a parent or guardian access to a teen's conversations, chat history, or message content. A parent can see when a linked account was last active, but not what was said.

A teen account is created automatically: anyone who enters a date of birth under 18 during sign-up is marked as a Teen account. A small "Teen" badge appears on their profile, and a set of default protections is switched on immediately — before any family member is even linked.

Link your accounts

Either the parent/guardian or the teen can start the invitation, from Settings > Parental controls.

Invite a family member

  1. Open Settings from your profile.
  2. Select Parental controls.
  3. Select Invite family member.
  4. Enter the other person's email address (or their phone number, if you'd rather invite that way).
  5. Choose whether they are your child or your parent/guardian.
  6. Select Send.

If the invite is sent to an email address that hasn't signed up yet, it's held and automatically delivered to their inbox the moment they create a Kashish AI account with that email. A phone-based invite works the same way, matched against the phone number on the recipient's account.

The other person sees the invite under Pending invites in their own Parental controls screen, with Accept and Decline options. Once accepted, both accounts appear under Family members for each other.

Good to know You can link with more than one family member — for example, a parent can be linked with several of their teens, and a teen can add more than one guardian if needed.

Manage parental controls

Once a teen account is linked, its default protections are visible from both sides in Settings > Parental controls:

SettingWhat it doesDefault
Mature & sensitive content filterBlocks sexual, violent, or graphic replies in chat.Enabled
Romantic / flirty companion modeTurns off flirty or romantic roleplay in replies, even if the teen initiates it.Enabled
Strong language filterBlocks profanity and strong language in replies.Enabled
Late-night use reminderA one-time gentle nudge to take a break if it's very late for the teen.Enabled
Restrict content (any linked account)A separate, parent-controlled switch that keeps every reply strictly clean — available for any linked family member, not only teens.Off

The first four rows are Teen protections — they turn on automatically for every Teen account at sign-up, whether or not a parent has linked up yet. They work differently from the "Restrict content" switch below them, which a parent can turn on or off directly at any time for anyone they supervise.

Removing a teen protection: request & approve

Teen protections can't be switched off from inside the app by the teen themselves, and a linked parent can't switch them off with a single tap either — each one is removed only through a request that the teen sends and a parent approves:

  1. The teen opens Settings > Parental controls and finds Your protections.
  2. Next to a protection that's still on, they select Request removal. (This only works once at least one parent/guardian is linked.)
  3. The request shows as Requested on the teen's side, and appears under that teen's name on the parent's Parental controls screen.
  4. The parent selects Accept to turn that one protection off, or Decline to keep it on. Declining doesn't block the teen from asking again later.

Each of the four Teen protections is requested and approved separately — accepting one request doesn't affect the other three. This is deliberately more restrictive than the "Restrict content" switch above, which a parent manages directly without a request step.

About the sensitive-content safeguards

The Mature & sensitive content filter and Romantic/flirty companion mode work together to keep a Teen account's conversations age-appropriate: they steer Kashish AI away from sexual or romantic roleplay, graphic or violent content, and away from initiating anything flirtatious, regardless of how the conversation is going.

Turning one of these off only changes that specific safeguard — it doesn't turn a Teen account into an adult account, remove the Teen badge, or affect any other protection.

Safety notifications

Please read this section carefully Kashish AI does not currently monitor conversations for self-harm, disordered eating, or other safety concerns, and it does not send automatic safety alerts to a linked parent or guardian. Linking accounts only shares the settings and "last active" information described on this page — nothing about what was actually said in a chat.

If you're a parent or guardian worried about a teen's wellbeing, the most reliable thing you can do is talk with them directly, and involve a doctor, school counselor, or mental health professional if you're concerned. If you believe someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services right away.

Unlink your accounts

Either person can remove the link at any time from Settings > Parental controls, by selecting the family member and confirming Remove this family link. This immediately ends the parent's ability to view or manage that account's settings.

Unlinking does not change a teen's existing protections — they stay exactly as they were (on, or previously removed) until a new parent/guardian is linked and future requests are approved.

When a teen turns 18

Current limitation Kashish AI does not yet automatically move an account out of Teen protections when someone turns 18. The Teen badge and its default protections are set once, from the date of birth entered at sign-up, and don't change on their own afterward.

If someone's Teen protections should no longer apply, the same request-and-approve flow above is the way to remove them today. Automatic age transition is something we're aware isn't built yet.

FAQ

Can a parent read a teen's conversations?

No. Linking accounts never shares chat content — only the protection settings on this page and a last-active timestamp.

Can a teen unlink parental controls on their own?

Yes. Either side can unlink at any time from Parental controls; there's no approval needed to unlink.

Does turning off "Restrict content" affect the four Teen protections?

No — they're independent. "Restrict content" is a separate, directly-toggled switch a parent controls for anyone they supervise; the four Teen protections can only change through a request the teen sends and the parent approves.

Can I link with more than one parent or more than one teen?

Yes, on both sides — there's no limit on how many family members you can link with.

Can I invite a family member by phone number instead of email?

Yes — Invite family member supports either an email address or a phone number, whichever is easier to reach the other person with.

Getting started with Kashish AI

What Kashish AI is, how to create an account, and how to send your first message or voice note.

Updated: September 2026

What is Kashish AI?

Kashish AI is a general-purpose AI companion you can chat or talk with for everyday things — conversation, advice, planning, writing help, brainstorming, or just company. It lives alongside a growing set of built-in tools (health tracking, reminders, weather, space, movies, a coding assistant called Codex, and more) that you can reach from the home menu without leaving the app.

Kashish AI shares its account system with Nexor Sparks, the social app where people post "Pulses," follow each other, and build a following — which is why a single account works across both.

Create your account

  1. Open Kashish AI and select Create account from the landing screen.
  2. Enter your full name and choose a username.
  3. Enter your email address.
  4. Enter your date of birth. You must be at least 13 to sign up, and this date is also what determines whether your account becomes a Teen account — see Family & teen safety.
  5. Choose a password.
  6. Review and agree to the Consumer Terms and Privacy Policy.
  7. Pick a plan — Free or Pro — to finish setup.

Already have an account? Select Login, then enter your email or username followed by your password.

Your first chat

Type into the message box at the bottom of the chat screen and send. Kashish AI keeps track of the conversation as a session, listed in the sidebar, so you can pick up where you left off or start a fresh one at any time.

You can attach a photo or take one with your camera directly from the chat screen, and Kashish AI will consider it as part of your message.

Using voice mode

Voice mode lets you talk out loud instead of typing. Tap the microphone to start; Kashish AI shows a live transcript of what it hears and replies out loud once you stop speaking.

Good to know Voice settings — including which voice is used and whether a voice reply is sent automatically when you stop talking — live under Settings > Voice. See Settings & data controls for details.

Explore the rest of the app

The home menu is the fastest way to reach everything else Kashish AI can do: Health, Reminders, Daily tasks, Bucket list, Images, Discover (weather, space, movies, hotels, food, medicine), and Codex. Each has its own help article linked below.

FAQ

Is Kashish AI free to use?

Yes — the Free plan covers core chat and voice use. Pro adds extra capability; see Account, profile & plans.

What's the minimum age to sign up?

13, subject to any higher minimum age required by the laws of your region.

Can I use Kashish AI in a language other than English?

Yes — see Settings & data controls for how to change your language.

Account, profile & plans

Your profile, logging in, the difference between Free and Pro, and how to upgrade.

Updated: September 2026

Your profile

Your profile shows your display name, username, and photo, plus a Teen badge if your account is a Teen account. Update your name or photo any time from Profile > Edit, then select Save.

Logging in

Sign in with either your email address or your username, plus your password. If you're switching devices, the same credentials work anywhere.

Can't sign in or didn't get a verification email? Write to auth@kashishai.com.

Free vs. Pro

PlanWhat you get

ProUnlimited chat and images, priority replies, and the Kashi 3, Kashi 4 and Kashi Mars models included. See Kashi models.

Your current plan is shown as a pill on the home screen and can be changed at any time from Settings > Upgrade.

Upgrading or changing your plan

  1. Open Settings from your profile.
  2. Select Upgrade.
  3. Compare the Free and Pro cards and select the one you want.
  4. Confirm your choice.

If an upgrade doesn't go through, an error is shown on the same screen — try again, or check your payment details before retrying.

FAQ

Can I downgrade from Pro back to Free?

Yes, from the same Settings > Upgrade screen.

Does my Teen badge ever change automatically?

No — see When a teen turns 18 in Family & teen safety for the current limitation.

Where do I turn off personalization or memory?

Under Settings > Personalization and Settings > Memory — covered in Chat, voice & memory.

Chat, voice & memory

Choosing a model, attaching photos, talking out loud, and how personalization and memory work.

Updated: September 2026

Choosing a model & effort level

The model pill above the message box shows which model is currently active. Tap it to open the model picker and choose a different one, and use the effort toggle next to it to trade off speed against how much the model "thinks" before answering.

See Kashi models & Kashi TY 4.5 for the full lineup.

Photos & attachments

Attach an existing photo or take one with your camera directly from the chat screen; a preview appears above the message box before you send. Kashish AI considers the image as part of your message alongside anything you type.

Any images you've generated or sent are also saved to your Library, where they're organized into a gallery you can revisit later.

Voice mode

Tap the microphone to start talking. Kashish AI shows a live transcript while you speak and replies out loud once you stop. A few voice settings live under Settings > Voice:

SettingWhat it does
Voice selectionChoose which voice Kashish AI speaks with, and preview it before saving.
Natural voiceUses a more expressive, less robotic speaking style.
Auto-sendSends your voice message automatically once you stop speaking, instead of waiting for you to confirm.
Voice hapticsAdds a light vibration when voice mode starts and stops listening.

Personalization & custom instructions

Custom instructions let you tell Kashish AI a bit about yourself so its replies fit you better — your nickname, your occupation, and how you'd like it to respond (tone, format, level of detail). Set these from Settings > Personalization; each one is optional and can be cleared at any time.

Good to know Personalization only shapes how Kashish AI responds — it doesn't share anything you enter here with other people, including linked family members.

Memory

When memory is turned on, Kashish AI can carry small facts you've mentioned from one conversation into another, so you don't have to repeat yourself. A running summary of what's been remembered is visible under Settings > Memory, where you can review it, delete individual items, or clear it entirely.

Turn memory off completely from the same screen — existing chats aren't deleted, but nothing further will be remembered across sessions.

FAQ

Does turning off memory delete my past conversations?

No — it only stops new facts from being carried forward. Your chat history is unaffected.

Can I delete a single memory without clearing everything?

Yes — each item under Settings > Memory can be removed individually.

Do family members linked through Parental controls see my personalization settings or memories?

No. As explained in Family & teen safety, linking only shares protection settings and a last-active timestamp — never chat content, personalization, or memory.

Health & wellness tracking

Steps, hydration, sleep, weight, mood, and the built-in breathing exercise — and how this data is handled.

Updated: September 2026

Overview

The Health screen brings together everyday wellness tracking in one place: three daily rings for movement, hydration, and mindfulness, plus dedicated sections for steps, water, sleep, weight, workouts, and mood.

Activity & steps

Turn on step tracking from the Health screen to start counting; your progress shows as a number and feeds the Move ring, alongside an estimate of calories burned. The Hydrate and Mindful rings track your water intake and mindfulness minutes for the day the same way.

Hydration & sleep

Log water with a tap each time you drink; the day's total fills a progress bar toward your goal. For sleep, enter your bedtime and wake time and Kashish AI calculates your total sleep and keeps a history so you can see trends over time.

Weight, BMI & nutrition

Enter your height once and log your weight over time to see a history and your current BMI. For nutrition, search for a food or a workout to see calories, and macros (carbs, protein, fat) are tallied for the day as you log meals.

Mood & the breathing exercise

Log your mood with a quick tap whenever you'd like; a history view shows how it's trended. The built-in breathing exercise walks you through a guided inhale-hold-exhale cycle with an on-screen animation — open it from Health whenever you want a short mindful break.

Who can see your health data

Please read this section carefully Health & wellness data — steps, water, sleep, weight, workouts, and mood — is yours alone. It is never shared with a linked parent, guardian, or teen through Parental controls, and it isn't used as part of any safety-notification system, because Kashish AI doesn't currently have one (see Family & teen safety).

FAQ

Do I need a wearable device to track steps?

No — step tracking uses your phone once you turn it on from the Health screen.

Can I edit or delete a logged entry?

Yes — open the relevant history (weight, sleep, or mood) and select the entry you want to change or remove.

Is any of this shown to other people?

No — see Who can see your health data above.

Productivity: reminders, tasks & bucket list

Keep track of daily to-dos, set reminders for a specific time, and hold onto longer-term goals.

Updated: September 2026

Daily tasks

Add a task from the Daily tasks screen and check it off once it's done. Tasks here are meant for same-day to-dos and reset each day, so it's a good place for the small things you want to get through today.

Reminders

  1. Open Reminders from the home menu.
  2. Enter what you want to be reminded about.
  3. Set the date and time.
  4. Select Save.

Unlike daily tasks, reminders are tied to a specific date and time and will notify you when that time arrives (as long as notifications are enabled — see Settings & data controls).

Bucket list

The bucket list is for longer-term goals with no due date — places to visit, things to try, or milestones you're working toward. Add an item any time and check it off whenever you get there.

FAQ

What's the difference between daily tasks and reminders?

Daily tasks are simple same-day checkboxes with no notification. Reminders are scheduled for an exact date and time and can notify you.

Can I edit a reminder after I've set it?

Yes — open it from the Reminders list and update the text or the date and time.

Discover: weather, space, movies, hotels, food & medicine

A set of lookup tools built into Kashish AI, separate from chat.

Updated: September 2026

Weather

Search for any city to see current conditions. Weather doesn't require any setup — just search and go.

Space

Space includes NASA's daily Astronomy Picture of the Day with its explanation, plus a searchable grid of planets and other bodies in our solar system. Select any body to open a detail sheet, including a 360° image viewer for supported bodies.

Movies

Browse and search movies by region. Movie data is powered by a third-party catalog (TMDB) — enter your own API key under Settings > Movies to enable this section.

Hotels

Search hotels for a destination. Hotel data is powered by a third-party travel API (Amadeus) — enter your own API key and secret under Settings > Hotels to enable this section.

Food & medicine search

Search for a food to see its nutrition information — useful alongside the nutrition tracking described in Health & wellness. Medicine search looks up information about a medicine by name.

Not medical advice Medicine search results are informational only and don't replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist. Always check with a medical professional before starting, stopping, or combining any medication.

FAQ

Why don't Movies or Hotels show any results?

Both need a personal API key entered under their respective Settings page first — see Movies and Hotels above.

Is my API key shared with anyone else?

No — it's stored on your account for your own use of that feature only.

Codex — the built-in AI coding assistant

Chat with an assistant that writes and previews HTML, CSS, and JavaScript live, right inside Kashish AI.

Updated: September 2026

Overview

Codex is a separate chat surface tuned for coding. Describe what you want to build, and Codex writes HTML, CSS, and JavaScript into its own editor, which you can preview instantly.

Chatting with Codex

Type a request into the Codex chat input — for example, "build me a countdown timer." Codex replies in the chat panel and updates the code editor to match.

The code editor & live preview

Codex's editor has three tabs — HTML, CSS, and JS — that you can also edit by hand. The preview panel re-renders your code live, so you can see the result as you or Codex make changes.

Console & history

The console shows any errors or logged output from your code, which is useful for debugging. The history panel keeps previous versions of your project so you can look back at or restore an earlier version.

FAQ

Can Codex work with frameworks like React?

Codex is focused on plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in a single live-previewed page.

Can I export what Codex builds?

The HTML, CSS, and JS tabs contain the full source, which you can copy out at any time.

Settings & data controls

General appearance, notifications, language, typing, and what happens to your data.

Updated: September 2026

General

Switch between light and dark theme, and pick an accent color, from Settings > General. A haptics switch controls whether the app vibrates lightly on certain interactions.

Notifications

Under Settings > Notifications, control whether you get a notification for new chat messages and whether a sound plays with it. All notifications also collect in the in-app notification center, reachable from the bell icon.

Language

Kashish AI currently supports English and Hindi. Change the app's language from Settings > Language; your choice applies immediately across the app.

Typing

Under Settings > General, you'll also find typing preferences: autocomplete (suggests the rest of a word as you type), autocorrect (fixes common typos automatically), and auto-language-switch (detects and switches keyboard language as you type).

Voice

Voice-specific settings — voice selection, natural voice, auto-send, and voice haptics — are covered in Chat, voice & memory.

Data controls

Under Settings > Data controls you can manage:

SettingWhat it does
Web searchLets Kashish AI search the web when a reply would benefit from current information.
Trending topicsSurfaces trending topics you might want to chat about.
Help improve the modelAllows your conversations to be used to help improve Kashish AI's models.
LocationLets Kashish AI use your device location for things like local weather.

Each of these is optional and can be turned off independently at any time.

To get a copy of your data, or to ask us to delete your account and associated data, email help@kashishai.com.

FAQ

If I turn off "Help improve the model," does that delete past conversations?

No — it only stops future conversations from being used that way going forward.

Do notification settings affect Reminders?

Yes — Reminders rely on notifications being enabled to alert you at the scheduled time.

Plugins & built-in tools

Everything Kashish can do beyond chat: create images, talk out loud, explore space, check the weather, watch videos and more.

Updated: September 2026

Where to find them

Open the home menu and choose Plugins. The list is split into Built-in tools, which work today, and Connect your apps, which are coming soon. Your generated images, saved chats and planner live in Library.

Built-in tools

ToolWhat it does
AI CreateGenerate images from a prompt. Free accounts have daily limits; Pro has unlimited images.
Voice ChatTalk to Kashish out loud. See Chat, voice & memory.
IRISKashish's vision mode. See Kashish IRIS.

SpaceA NASA image and Astronomy Picture of the Day explorer. See Discover.
WeatherLive forecast lookup. See Discover.

Library

Library keeps your content in one place: Images (your AI-generated images), Saved chats (all your conversations) and Planner.

Connect your apps (coming soon)

These app connections are not available yet. They appear in the Plugins list marked Soon:

  • Instagram: chat about your DMs and posts
  • Facebook: Pages and Messenger from Kashish
  • Google: sign-in and account sync
  • Gmail: triage and draft replies in chat
  • Google Calendar: see and add events from chat
  • Outlook: mail and calendar in one place
  • Canva: create and edit designs
  • GitHub: repos, issues and pull requests in chat
  • Shopify: orders and store insights

FAQ

Can Kashish read my Gmail or calendar today?

Not yet. Gmail, Google Calendar and the other app connections are coming soon.

Where do my generated images go?

They are saved to Library > Images so you can revisit them later.

Is there a limit on images?

On the Free plan, image generation has daily limits. Kashish Pro has unlimited images.

Kashish IRIS smart glasses

AI smart glasses powered end to end by Kashish AI. See more, know more, do more.

Updated: September 2026

Current status

IRIS is in development and the release date is to be announced. Pre-orders open country by country, so availability depends on where you live.

What IRIS does

  • Capture life, hands-free. A built-in camera in the frame for photos and video, with no phone needed.
  • Kashish AI on the go. The same assistant you chat with in the app, hands-free.
  • Calls and notifications. Stay connected without pulling out your phone.
  • All-day power. Built to last a full day of normal use on a single charge.

IRIS is also the name of Kashish's vision mode inside the app.

How to pre-book

  1. Open Kashish and log in.
  2. Open IRIS from the home menu.
  3. Select Pre-book IRIS.
  4. Select your country or region. We check availability first, and you are not charged at this step.
  5. Enter your contact email.
  6. Review the details and select Place pre-order.

If IRIS is not available in your country yet, you will see a message saying pre-orders have not opened there. You have not been charged and nothing was placed.

FAQ

Am I charged when I check availability?

No. We check availability for your country first, and you are not charged at that step.

When will IRIS be released?

The release date is to be announced.

I have a question about my pre-order.

Email help@kashishai.com and include the email you used to pre-book.

Contact us in the app

You can also reach the Nexor Sparks support team through the app. To open Kashish, go to kashishai.com and log in.

What to include

  • The email or username you signed up with.
  • Your device and whether you use the app or a browser.
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot, if it helps.

Never send your password or one-time codes. We will never ask for them.

Reporting a concern & safety

If you see something that breaks the Acceptable Use Policy or a feature feels unsafe, email help@kashishai.com to report it. If you believe an action on your account was a mistake, use the same address to request a review.

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline right away. Kashish is not equipped to handle emergencies.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers that cut across account, chat, family, health, and privacy topics.

Updated: September 2026

Account & plans

How do I change my display name or photo?

From Profile > Edit — see Account, profile & plans.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Pro has unlimited chat and images, priority replies, and the Kashi 3, Kashi 4 and Kashi Mars models included. On Free, chat and images have daily limits and those models use more credits. See Account, profile & plans.

Chat, voice & memory

Can I turn off personalization and memory?

Yes, independently, from Settings > Personalization and Settings > Memory.

Can I change Kashish AI's voice?

Yes, from Settings > Voice — see Chat, voice & memory.

Family & teen safety

Can a parent read a teen's chats?

No — see Family & teen safety for exactly what is and isn't shared.

Is my health data shared with anyone?

No — see Who can see your health data in Health & wellness tracking.
