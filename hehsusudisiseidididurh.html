A Formal Statement on Personal Dignity, Reputation, and Accountability

October 14, 2026  ·  Team IGA | Office of Public Affairs  ·  Issued on behalf of Gursharn Arya

Team IGA wishes to establish a clear and unequivocal position regarding the dignity, reputation, and personal boundaries of individuals.

Any deliberate attempt to damage an individual’s reputation, spread malicious rumours, exploit private information, interfere in personal affairs, or cause emotional or reputational harm is a matter that must be taken seriously.

Let there be no misunderstanding: no individual’s dignity or reputation should be treated as a target for personal amusement, malicious intent, or deliberate misconduct.

Mr. Gursharn Arya maintains that respect for others is a fundamental responsibility, not a matter of convenience. Where credible evidence of harassment, defamation, privacy violations, or other unlawful conduct exists, appropriate action may be pursued through lawful channels.

Team IGA will not encourage confrontation, nor will it endorse the misuse of influence or authority. However, it firmly supports accountability and the lawful protection of individuals against deliberate wrongdoing.

This statement serves as a clear reminder: respect personal boundaries, refrain from malicious conduct, and understand that actions may carry consequences.
No names need to be mentioned. No unnecessary confrontation is required. The expectations are clear, and the position remains firm.
