Nexor Technologies started on the Stanford University campus and today runs three products. Two of them, MarsX and MarsX Business, are credited by the company with driving its valuation growth. The charts below show where the valuation has been, what the 2030 target requires, and who has backed the company.

1. The valuation path

Nexor began 2023 at a low valuation, rose sharply through 2025 and climbed much higher in 2026. October 2026 brought a steep fall, followed by a recovery to $151 million in November.
