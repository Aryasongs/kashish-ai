Update on Founder Gursharn Arya’s Health

17 November 2026 · Nexor Technologies

17 NOVEMBER 2026 — Nexor Technologies today shared a brief update on the health of its Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Gursharn Arya (Gursharan Singh). Mr. Arya has been unwell for the past few days with a persistent fever and is currently resting and recovering.

Business continues as usual

The company’s products and services, including Kashish AI, marsX, marsX Business, Nexor Sparks, Lumi and Montè, continue to operate normally. The leadership team and the Board of Directors remain in close contact and are carrying on with day-to-day work and planned initiatives, including the company’s recently announced investment in Montè.

A request for privacy

Mr. Arya and his family are grateful for the kind wishes received so far. They ask that their privacy be respected during this time, and that readers rely only on official statements from Nexor Technologies rather than unverified messages or social media posts. Further updates will be shared by the company if and when there is something important to report.

Nexor Technologies and its team wish Mr. Arya a speedy and complete recovery.
