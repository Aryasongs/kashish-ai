6. Gmail and Google data

This section applies only if you choose to connect your Gmail account. If you never connect Gmail, none of it affects you.

6.1 What you are agreeing to

When you tap Connect next to Gmail, Google shows its own consent screen. If you approve, Kashish receives a temporary access token for one permission only: read-only access to your Gmail (the gmail.readonly scope). Connecting is always your choice and is never required to use Kashish.

6.2 What Kashish does with your Gmail

Kashish willKashish will never
Read your messages only when you ask, for example “summarize my unread emails” or “what did Rahul send me today?” Send, reply to, forward, delete, archive, label, or change any email.
Pass the emails relevant to your request to the AI service that writes the answer, so it can summarize or explain them to you. Sell your Google user data or share it with advertisers, data brokers, or information resellers.
Show the result to you in the chat, and nothing more. Use your Gmail content for advertising, including personalized or retargeted ads.
Request a fresh token each session. Tokens are short-lived. Use your Gmail content to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Drafting an email in Kashish is separate from the Gmail connection. When Kashish drafts a message, it only opens it in your own mail app for you to review and send yourself.

Kashish’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, that means we use your Gmail data only to provide the features you asked for in the App, we don’t allow humans to read it unless you give us specific permission, it is needed for security or abuse investigation, or the law requires it, and we don’t transfer it to others except as needed to provide the feature, to follow the law, or as part of a merger or sale where you are notified.
