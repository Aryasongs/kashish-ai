Questions, answered.

What is Kashish AI?

Kashish is an AI companion you can chat with by text, talk to by voice, or just hang out with. It remembers what matters to you between conversations.

Is Kashish free?

Yes. The Free plan includes chat, voice, and image generation with daily limits. Kashish Pro adds unlimited chat and images, priority replies, and the Kashi 3, Kashi 4, and Kashi Mars models.

What are Kashish IRIS smart glasses?

IRIS are AI smart glasses with a built-in camera, hands-free Kashish AI, and calls and notifications. They are still in development and have no release date yet. Pre-booking costs a $100 fee, and the price is $471.99 for your region. You are not charged when availability is checked for your country.

Who can use Kashish?

You must be 13 or older to create an account. Accounts for teens have age-appropriate settings.

What is Kashi TY 4.5?

Kashi TY 4.5 is the next Kashish model, launching on 24 October 2026.

Where can I use Kashish?

Kashish runs on the web, iOS, and Android. Codex, the code assistant, needs a laptop or desktop screen.
