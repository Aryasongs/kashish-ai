Nexor Technologies Commits Major Time and Capital to Montè, Setting Its Sights on the Luxury Market

Premium fabrics, a focused design philosophy and board-level backing mark a new chapter for the Nexor fashion house.

16 November 2026 · Nexor Technologies
Nexor Technologies / Newsroom / Press release

16 NOVEMBER 2026 — Nexor Technologies today announced a strategic commitment to invest significant time, capital and leadership attention in Montè, its clothing brand, with the aim of establishing Montè as a recognised name in luxury fashion. The decision was endorsed by the Board of Directors and is led by Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Gursharan Singh, professionally known as Gursharn Arya. It makes fashion a core pillar of the Nexor ecosystem, alongside its technology products.

A new priority for Nexor Technologies

Nexor Technologies is best known for Kashish AI, marsX, marsX Business, Nexor Sparks and Lumi. Montè, which launched as “The Signature Collection”, has so far been a quiet part of that portfolio. With this announcement the company is elevating it from a single-collection label into a long-term luxury house.

Nexor will dedicate design, sourcing, quality-control and brand teams to Montè, and will direct growing investment towards premium raw materials, pattern development, production quality, brand identity, packaging, customer experience and entry into new markets.

Fabric first

Montè’s approach begins with the material. Its current collection is cut from premium, soft-touch cotton in a relaxed, oversized silhouette that looks refined and feels effortless. Under the new plan, Nexor will deepen that commitment:

Superior fibresTrusted mills and suppliers, with inspection at every stage.
Finishing standardsStitching, wash treatments, pattern accuracy and consistent fit.
Considered rangeFocused collections in the spirit of quiet luxury, not volume.
TraceabilityClear records of where materials come from and how garments are made.

Designed for everyone, finished like luxury

Montè offers sizes S to 4XL for women, men and kids, reflecting a belief that premium design should be inclusive. That principle stays as the brand moves upmarket. Existing customer benefits, including free shipping across India, cash on delivery, the M Coins rewards programme and early access to drops through MONTÈ+, will continue and expand as the brand grows.

Statement from the Founder

“Montè was created with a simple idea: clothing should look calm, feel exceptional and last. Today we are putting our time, our resources and our full attention behind that idea. We want Montè to stand beside the world’s respected fashion houses, and we will earn that place through fabric, craftsmanship and patience.”Gursharan Singh (Gursharn Arya), Founder, Chairman and CEO, Nexor Technologies

Backed by the Board of Directors

The Montè plan was reviewed and endorsed by the Nexor Technology Board of Directors, which brings experience across corporate strategy, finance, technology, operations, governance, marketing and brand development. Members with finance and risk backgrounds will oversee budgets and milestones, while those experienced in brand, marketing and international operations will guide positioning and market entry.

The board is chaired by Gursharan Singh (Gursharn Arya), Founder, Chairman and CEO of Nexor Technologies. Full board details are available at kashishai.com/board-of-directors.

What happens next

  1. Expand the collection with new seasonal pieces in premium fabrics, announced to MONTÈ+ members first.
  2. Upgrade the brand experience through refined packaging, photography, storytelling and customer service.
  3. Strengthen the supply chain with additional quality checks and vetted manufacturing partners.
  4. Open new markets beyond India, subject to logistics, compliance and regulatory readiness.
  5. Connect the ecosystem by using Nexor Sparks, marsX and Kashish AI to bring customers closer to the brand.

About Montè

Montè is the clothing brand of Nexor Technologies, offering quiet-luxury essentials in premium soft cotton with a relaxed, oversized fit for women, men and kids. Shop at monte.kashishai.com.
