Valuation methodology

The framework below is used to assess a diversified technology group. The $163.75M figure is an estimated, management-provided equity valuation. The underlying methodology and independent verification status have not been disclosed.

1

Sum-of-the-Parts Valuation

Each portfolio business is valued on its own and the results are combined into a group figure.

2

Market Comparable Analysis

Valuation benchmarks from comparable listed and private technology companies.

3

Revenue Multiples

Revenue-based multiples applied where a business generates reportable revenue.

4

Discounted Cash Flow Analysis

Projected future cash flows discounted to present value.

5

Intellectual Property Assessment

Value attributed to brands, software, proprietary technology and digital assets.

6

Technology Asset Assessment

Review of platforms, infrastructure and product technology owned by the group.

7

Strategic Business Value

Value from the strategic fit of businesses within one ecosystem.
