Nexor Technologies to Invest $146 Million in India, Open New Offices and Build Digital Infrastructure

17 NOVEMBER 2026 — Nexor Technologies today announced plans to invest $146 million in India to open new offices and develop digital infrastructure that will power its growing family of products. The investment, led by Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Gursharn Arya (Gursharan Singh), is the company’s largest announced commitment to date and signals long-term confidence in India’s digital economy.

Key facts

Investment$146 million planned for India
FocusNew offices and digital infrastructure
Led byGursharn Arya (Gursharan Singh)
ProductsKashish AI, marsX, marsX Business, Nexor Sparks, Lumi, Montè, Kashish IRIS

Where the capital will go

Nexor Technologies will deploy the planned investment across two priorities: expanding its physical presence in India, and building the digital foundations that keep its products fast, secure and available to millions of people.

New officesEngineering, design, product, support and operations teams in India, closer to the users Nexor serves.
Cloud and computeScalable infrastructure to run Kashish AI, including voice, image and code features.
Secure messagingReliable, encrypted infrastructure for marsX and the marsX Business OTP and messaging API.
Social platformCapacity and tools for Nexor Sparks, including real-time posting, news and creator features.
Security and privacyStronger protection, monitoring and compliance practices across every product.
TalentHiring and training in AI, engineering, security, design and customer experience.

Why India, why now

India is one of the world’s largest and fastest-growing digital markets, with a young population that is adopting AI, messaging and social platforms at remarkable speed. Nexor Technologies, which builds products for these exact needs, sees an opportunity to grow with India’s digital ecosystem by investing locally in people and infrastructure rather than relying only on remote operations.

The company expects the investment to improve product speed and reliability, support local jobs and give Indian businesses and users access to dependable technology built with their needs in mind.

Statement from the Founder

“India is where we are building our future. This investment is about people and foundations: offices where talented teams can do their best work, and infrastructure that is secure, fast and ready for the next decade. We intend to build something lasting.”Gursharn Arya (Gursharan Singh), Founder, Chairman and CEO, Nexor Technologies

Board oversight

The investment plan was reviewed and endorsed by the Nexor Technology Board of Directors. The board, chaired by Gursharn Arya, will oversee budgets, milestones and governance as the programme moves forward. Board information is available at kashishai.com/board-of-directors.

What happens next

  1. Site selection. Nexor will evaluate locations for its new Indian offices and announce them as plans are confirmed.
  2. Infrastructure build-out. Cloud, compute, security and messaging capacity will be expanded in phases.
  3. Hiring. Teams will grow across engineering, AI, security, design, support and operations.
  4. Partnerships. The company will work with technology, telecom and business partners to extend reach.
  5. Progress updates. Nexor will share milestones through its official channels.

Vision: infrastructure that people can trust

Great products are built on foundations most people never see: data centres, networks, security systems, support teams and the engineers who keep everything running. Nexor Technologies believes that for AI, private messaging and social platforms to earn lasting trust, those foundations must be strong, secure and close to the people who depend on them.

The $146 million programme is designed around that belief. Rather than treating India as only a user market, Nexor intends to make it a place where products are designed, operated, protected and supported.

Digital infrastructure development: the five pillars

1 · ReliabilityRedundant systems, monitoring and recovery planning, so services stay available when demand rises.
2 · SpeedInfrastructure placed to reduce delay for chat, voice, image and messaging features.
3 · SecurityEncryption, access controls, audits and incident response built into every layer.
4 · ScaleCapacity planned in phases, so growth in users and businesses never outruns the platform.
5 · ResponsibilityPrivacy-first design, transparent policies and respect for user choice.

Benefits for users, businesses and creators

For everyday users, the investment is expected to mean faster, steadier apps and quicker help when something goes wrong. For businesses, stronger messaging and OTP infrastructure can support safer customer verification and clearer communication. For creators, a more capable social platform offers better tools to share, grow and earn.

Nexor also intends to keep the same principles across all products: clear pricing, honest labelling of AI, and respect for privacy.

Security, privacy and responsible AI

As Nexor expands, protecting people remains a first-order priority. The company plans to strengthen its security and privacy practices alongside its infrastructure, including regular security reviews, careful handling of personal data, clear user controls and responsible AI practices such as labelling AI-generated assistance.

  • Security reviews and testing as systems grow.
  • Privacy-by-design in new features and services.
  • Clear policies, transparent terms and accessible support.
  • Responsible AI, with sensible limits, safety checks and human oversight.

Offices, teams and talent

New Nexor offices will bring together engineering, AI, security, design, product, support and operations under one roof, so teams can collaborate faster and respond to users in their own context. The company plans to hire and train talent across these areas and to build a culture that rewards curiosity, craftsmanship and accountability.

Specific locations, hiring numbers and opening dates will be shared once they are confirmed.

Partnerships and ecosystem

Nexor intends to work with technology providers, telecom and connectivity partners, universities, startups and businesses to extend its reach. Through marsX Business, companies of all sizes will be able to connect with customers using verified, secure messaging, and through Nexor Sparks and Kashish AI, creators and developers will have more ways to build on the ecosystem.
