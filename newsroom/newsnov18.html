RAO KILL

Respect, Accountability & Ownership — Keep Intrusive, Lewd & Lecherous behaviour out

Zero Tolerance. Recorded. Enforced.

18 November 2026 · Nexor Technologies

18 NOVEMBER 2026 — Nexor Technologies today issued an official notice under its zero-tolerance policy: Respect, Accountability & Ownership — Keep Intrusive, Lewd & Lecherous behaviour out. Harassment, pursuit or unwelcome advances towards any Nexor employee will not be tolerated. Every message, call and gesture directed at an employee through any channel can be recorded, preserved as evidence and used to hold the person responsible, including through legal proceedings under applicable law.

Notice to all persons: if an employee has indicated that contact is unwelcome, it must stop immediately. Continuing is at your own legal risk.

01What the policy means

Respect for every person at Nexor. Accountability for anyone who crosses the line. Ownership of employee safety by the company and its leaders. Intrusive, lewd and lecherous behaviour has no place in our workplace, on our platforms or in our people’s lives.

What a person says and does towards a Nexor employee is their own responsibility, and it will be treated as such.

02Who is covered

  • Customers and users of Kashish AI, marsX, Nexor Sparks, Lumi, Montè and other Nexor products.
  • Vendors, contractors, partners, visitors and members of the public.
  • Colleagues and team members, who are also bound by workplace conduct rules and the law on workplace harassment.

03Conduct that will be acted on

  • Repeated or persistent romantic or sexual messages, proposals or compliments after the employee shows no interest.
  • Continued contact after being asked to stop.
  • Obscene, sexual or suggestive messages, images, voice notes or videos.
  • Pressure, bribery or threats to obtain a relationship or a response.
  • Stalking, tracking, fake accounts or impersonation.

Polite, professional communication about products, orders, support or business is always welcome. These policies target unwelcome conduct, not ordinary courtesy.

04The enforcement ladder

ReportThe employee or any witness reports the conduct, with screenshots, usernames, dates and records.
PreserveMessages and records from official channels are secured and handled as evidence.
Warn and restrictThe person is warned in writing, contact is blocked and accounts may be restricted or removed.
Legal noticeWhere conduct continues or is serious, a formal legal notice is issued through counsel.
Legal actionPolice complaint, court proceedings and every remedy available under applicable Indian law.

05Evidence and privacy

Records from official Nexor channels, such as customer support, company email, company social accounts and business lines, are preserved in line with policy and law. Employees are encouraged to keep their own screenshots and share them. Nexor does not read private end-to-end encrypted conversations between users on marsX; evidence there comes from what the affected person or reporter shares and from lawful processes.

06Statement from the Founder

“Our people come to work to build, not to be pursued or pressured. This policy is not a slogan. It is a promise that respect is non-negotiable and that those who cross the line will answer for it.”Gursharn Arya (Gursharan Singh), Founder, Chairman and CEO

07Fair process, no retaliation

Strict does not mean careless. Each complaint is examined on its facts, action is proportionate to what is found, and no one is punished on rumour. False or malicious complaints are also taken seriously. No employee who reports harassment will face retaliation of any kind.

08How to report

  • Email help@kashishai.com with a short description, usernames, dates and evidence.
  • Employees may also report to their manager or leadership.
  • If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
